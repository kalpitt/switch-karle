### 2.2 SD/PT and HRA renumbering

* SD+PT convergence on **s.19 ITA 2025 — Deductions from salary**: TaxGuru “[Section 19 – Deductions from salary (standard deduction, professional tax, etc.)](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/income-salary-income-tax-act-2025-practical-note-01-04-2026.html)”; CalcGuru “[Stated under ITA 2025 (Section 19) and ITA 1961 (Section 16)](https://calcguru.in/standard-deduction-salary/)”; TaxCorp “[Section 19 – Deductions from salary](https://thetaxcorp.in/article/salary-income-under-income-tax-act-2025-detailed-practical-guide-effective)”; Virtual Auditor groups SD+PT under s.19. CA-1’s s.57 = Income from other sources (mis-map). CA-3/4 “still 16(ia)/16(iii)” is transitional ITR-utility shorthand, not the Act.
* HRA convergence on **Schedule III Table Sl No 11 via s.11 ITA 2025** (formerly s.10(13A)+Rule 2A): Aryan “[Old s.10(13A) → Schedule III, Sr No 11, specified by s.11](https://www.aryanconsultancy.com/post/salary-allowances-and-perquisite-taxation-complete-changes-under-income-tax-act-2025-vs-1961)”; Zoho “[Schedule III (Table: S. No. 11) (formerly s.10(13A))](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/hra-exemption-calculator/)”. From Tax Year 2026-27, 50% limb expands to 8 cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai + Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) per **Income-tax Rules, 2026** (CBDT 20 Mar 2026, EY alert).

**Decision:** Cite **s.19** (SD+PT, formerly s.16(ia)/16(iii)) and **Schedule III Table Sl No 11 via s.11** (HRA, formerly s.10(13A)). Keep 4-city engine code but comment the 8-city expansion.

### 2.3 Rebate section & marginal relief

* Section: **s.156 ITA 2025** unanimous 4/4; s.157 is salary-arrears relief (s.89 equivalent — TaxTMI clause list 156 follows 155, precedes 157). Primary: [s.156](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-156-84). Two limbs: s.156(1) ₹12,500 to ₹5L; s.156(2)(a) ₹60k to ₹12L.
* Relief: **s.156(2)(b)** `rebate = Tax − (Income−12L)` ≡ `min(slabTax, excess)` is confirmed by CA-1/2/3 and Zoho sheet “Relief Under Section 156(2)(b)”. CA-4’s “zero above ₹5L, no partial rebate” conflates old 87A(1) with new s.156(2) and is rejected.

### 2.4 Surcharge cap

Primary: [Applicable Surcharge Rates](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1) table — new 50L 10% / 1Cr 15% / >2Cr **25% capped**; old adds 37% >5Cr. Virtual Auditor: “[25% above ₹2Cr — the old 37% rate has been abolished under the new regime](https://virtualauditor.in/learn/surcharge-cess-income-tax-2025/)”. Finance Act 2026 First Schedule and TAXAJ agree 37% is old-only. Code `surchargeRate` already correct. **CA-4’s 37% new-regime rejected.**

### 2.5 Professional Tax — Karnataka, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Bihar, Assam, Chhattisgarh

* **KA:** Primary `ptax.karnataka.gov.in/FAQ` — “PT for **Feb-2026** at Rs.300 instead of Rs.200 per Notif. DPAL 08 SHASANA 2025 dt 15.04.2025.” Amendment Act No.14/2023 (Mondaq/Lexology/SetIndiaBiz) — salaried ≥₹25k → **Rs.200/mo** (threshold up from ₹15k). Annual **₹2,400**; effective 2026 annual **₹2,500** (200×11+300). No Rs.300×12. **CA-1’s ₹3,600 rejected.**
* **PB:** Primary [PSDT Act 2018 Schedule](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/punjab/2018/2018PB11.pdf) — “Rs.200 per month” for salaries; flat ₹200/mo for IT-payers (>₹2.5L income), lump-sum ₹2,200/yr — [psdt.punjab.gov.in](https://psdt.punjab.gov.in/); [CalcGuru Punjab](https://calcguru.in/professional-tax-punjab/). * **UK:** Majority 2026 compilations (LabourCodes360, EzHRM, ClearlyComply, Navi, ET) list **UK among no-PT states**. UK comtax portal shows no PT Act. FactoHR UK slab is undated/2024 and uncorroborated. **CA-4’s ₹2,500 rejected; UK = 0.**
* **BR:** Primary [Greythr Bihar](https://www.greythr.com/wiki/acts/professional-tax-bihar/) and Indiasalary — annual slabs nil ≤3L / ₹1k 3–5L / ₹2k 5–10L / **₹2,500 >10L** → max 2,500. CA-4’s “per month” multiplies ×12 and breaches Art.276 ceiling.
* **AS:** Primary [Chillsoft](https://chillsoft.io/professional-tax-calculator.html) + Indiasalary — ₹150/₹180/₹208 → max **₹2,496**.
